# Dungeon pottery family

Production starts from main7b808473 on codex/dungeon-pottery. Only Object2/Object28,29,30 BMD replacements and this deliverable folder are owned; Object28 and Object29 installed after independent final acceptance. All textures remain frozen. Lorencia's final offline gate is separately recorded by the coordinator; it does not confer client verification on Dungeon work.

The first prototype is Object29, an octagonal painted storage jar with70 exterior/base triangles and eight deep_wall04 interior cone triangles. The secondary material is the actual pot throat, not ground debris. It remains exact. The design adds a rolled ceramic lip outside its preserved mouth interface and a continuous curved belly/shoulder, maintaining the original painted band coordinates. The original bottom faces and ground contact polygon remain unchanged. New vertices are bounded by every actual placement's supporting planes so pitched placements do not gain a new ground penetration or footprint. This constraint protects actual extrema rather than treating every original intermediate vertex as an immutable artistic control.

Object29 has11 placements, including index75 pitched65degrees at scale1.54 and other records up to1.68. Object28 has24 and Object30 has10, giving45 total. Their baseline rigs are distinct Cylinder03/05/04 roots, each one action/key with lock0. Raw model names are legacy CP949 bytes; official importer/exporter receive a focused metadata adapter that reads ASCII action records and passes original name bytes to the supported converter --name option. Geometry/UV/rig processing stays in the official Blender/SourceTools pipeline. Full decrypted32byte name fields are audited, not merely converter equivalence.

Frozen Object2/flower_vase.OZJ is dedicated to these three models. Object2/deep_wall04.OZJ has14 model consumers and is frozen. Both original containers and decoded payloads are retained; packed source images must equal these payload bytes.

Independent component inspection found three whole pots in Object28 with matching UV/winding topology to29, but one is nonuniformly narrow/tall. Actual affine corner correspondence must be proved before any propagation. Object28/30 also share one broken part; another differs by six exterior faces. No generic whole-model or uniform-scale substitution is authorized by resemblance.

Required acceptance evidence: original/current references, REF_ORIGINAL packed source, official export and actual reimport, complete authored position/material/UV/bone/cyclic-winding match, raw normal world-direction match, unchanged rig/actions/material order/name bytes, exact protected throat/base faces, actual placement bounds, diffuse front/reverse/reduced/wire views, and placed context. Only independently accepted candidates may be installed or committed as game replacements. All renders are offline; client review remains pending.

## Family candidate

Object29's accepted direction was reduced from 782 to 398 triangles without losing the rounded shoulder or rolled rim. Both prototypes are retained under studies. The final family source also includes the transformed support directions from all 24 Object28 placements. A direct affine copy without these constraints expanded one rotated bound by 0.556 model units; the constrained family now preserves Object29 placed bounds exactly at exported precision and Object28 bounds within 0.000107 units.

Object28 contains three whole instances: full size, a narrow anisotropic variant (about 0.641/0.641/1), and a small uniform variant (about 0.6024). All 78 original corners/material/UV/winding signatures fit each affine transform within 0.0001 units. The replacement has 1476 triangles: three 398-triangle canonical pots plus 282 original broken-part triangles. Its 324 protected triangles comprise those broken parts and the three exact original base/throat surfaces. Exported-variant proof accounts for every triangle, separately bounds position and UV errors, and preserves the distinct original root names.

Object30 remains byte-identical to baseline. Independent direction review finds the broken rims already have physical thickness and purposeful jagged outlines; the broken parts remain consistent with Object28. Their shared 174-triangle component has exact UV/material/cyclic topology and an affine position fit. The other broken component shares 108 triangles, with six extra original bottom triangles in Object30. No broken geometry is silently replaced with the whole-pot recipe. Independent final review accepts Object28 and Object29 at the exact candidate-manifest hashes and explicitly retains Object30 baseline. The report is retained in independent-final-review.json.

The initial source-match report's precision note was inherited from the earlier 320-unit Wells audit; that sentence describes the prior codec-budget rationale, not the size of these pots. The measured errors in each report are from these actual exported pots. The audit script now states the separate 0.0003 position and 0.000001 UV budgets directly; results, tolerances and candidate hashes are unchanged.

Normal/UV audits retain explicit legacy exceptions rather than claiming every original face was clean: Object28's untouched broken interior face 506 becomes candidate face 1466 and retains its opposite-facing corner normal; Object30's original face 278 remains unchanged with the retained game file. Original degenerate-UV bottom/fragment faces are accepted only through exact baseline material/position/UV/bone/cyclic correspondence. All newly authored surfaces must pass positive corner-normal incidence and nondegenerate UV/geometry checks.
