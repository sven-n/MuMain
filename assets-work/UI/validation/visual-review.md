# Offline visual review — 2026-09-22

Inspected the retained original atlas, generated paintings, decoded-export
before/after at native logical size, 4× inspection crops, both 1920×1080 mockups
and light/dark background sheets. These are offline mockups, not client captures.

- The familiar four symbols are recognizable in the same control positions.
  The painted backgrounds are quieter and darker than the original patterned
  insets. Small metal highlights remain readable after downsampling.
- Hover is brighter and warmer; selected/pressed is recessed/darker; selected
  hover retains the brighter symbol. Cell positions and outer rails align.
- Borders do not expose light-colored transparent fringes on the light sheet.
  Black backing is visibly opaque, as the JPEG loader requires.
- The panel change is intentionally small: painted green stone in the empty
  skill well plus narrow trim. Gauge wells, XP trough, split guards and black
  control backing remain original. The green well still reads as the same slot.
- The original left/center HUD and cash-shop button remain visibly from the old
  material pass. That is the pilot's declared scope, not a completed global skin.
- The wide mockup preserves gaps between separately anchored HUD bands. It does
  not stretch the entire 640-pixel HUD across the screen.

No claim is made about client input behavior, GPU rendering, timing of blinking,
actual gameplay readability in motion, or HiDPI content scale. Those remain for
the pending client session listed in `../notes.md`.
