# Trees01 painted artwork prompts

All artwork generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Raw PNGs are retained; packaging resizes and pads transparent RGB without changing generated alpha.

## Tree_a

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: RGBA diffuse foliage cutout texture for spreading oak crowns in classic MU Online Lorencia. Square flat texture painting, straight front-facing view. An irregular branching spray of many small broad oak-like leaves, clustered into about five medium leaf groups, distributed over most of square with plenty of irregular holes. True transparency should occupy roughly 45-50 percent of square and foliage/stems roughly 50-55 percent. Silhouette reaches near all four edges without forming a square border. Dark muted olive, desaturated gray-green, brown twig structure, occasional restrained dry ochre leaf tips. Painterly medieval fantasy diffuse artwork: bold readable clusters, softly painted leaf veins and subtle edges, deep forest recesses, no photoreal noise, no neon green, no PBR glare. This texture repeats on many crown cards and should look like connected tree branches with broad leaves, not an isolated whole tree or random floating leaf scatter. Fine branching twigs connect the groups. No background, soil, sky, trunk, pot, cast shadow, text, frame or watermark. Real transparent alpha required. 1024×1024 intended to downsample to512×512.
